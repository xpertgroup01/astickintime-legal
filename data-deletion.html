Data Deletion Instructions — A Stick In Time

Last updated: 10 November 2026

The A Stick In Time Meta app is an internal tool used only to manage the channel’s own Facebook Page and Instagram account (@astickintimeofficial). It does not collect or store personal data about followers, viewers or commenters. Its records are limited to the channel’s own publishing activity (such as post IDs and publishing times) and an access token held in an encrypted vault.

How to request deletion of your data

If you believe any data relating to you is held in connection with this app, you can have it deleted in three steps:

  1. Send an email to astickintimeofficial@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include your Facebook or Instagram username and a short note identifying the data you want deleted.
  3. We will delete any data associated with you within 30 days and confirm completion by reply email.

How to revoke the app’s access completely

The account owner can end the app’s access at any moment, without contacting us:

  1. In Meta Business Settings, open Users → System Users and revoke or delete the app’s access token, or
  2. In Facebook Settings, open Apps and Websites (or Business Integrations) and remove the app.

Revocation takes effect immediately: the app can no longer read or publish anything on the accounts, and its stored token becomes useless.

Contact

Questions about data deletion: astickintimeofficial@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
