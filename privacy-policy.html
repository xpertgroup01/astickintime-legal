Privacy Policy — A Stick In Time

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Who we are

A Stick In Time is a YouTube, Facebook and Instagram channel that publishes educational animated videos. This policy covers the A Stick In Time Meta app (the “App”), an internal tool used only to manage the channel’s own Facebook Page and Instagram account. Contact: astickintimeofficial@gmail.com.

2. What the App does

The App connects to Meta’s Graph API to publish the channel’s own videos and Reels to the A Stick In Time Facebook Page and the @astickintimeofficial Instagram account, and to read performance statistics for those same accounts. It is operated solely by the channel owner. It has no public sign-up, no user accounts, and no other users.

3. Data the App accesses

Through the Meta Graph API, the App accesses only data belonging to the channel’s own accounts: the Facebook Page name and ID, the Instagram account name and ID, follower and media counts, metadata of the channel’s own posts and videos, and aggregated performance statistics (Insights) for those accounts.

The App does not access personal profiles, friend lists, private messages, or personal data about followers, viewers or commenters. Insights data provided by Meta is aggregated and anonymised.

4. Data we store

The App’s access token is held in an encrypted secrets vault and is never published or shared. Operational records are limited to the channel’s own publishing activity, such as post IDs and publishing times, which the channel owner uses to keep a publishing log. We store no personal data about followers, viewers or any other individual.

5. How data is used

Data accessed through the App is used only to publish the channel’s own content and to prepare performance reports for the channel owner. We do not sell data, share data with third parties, use data for advertising, or use data for any purpose unrelated to running the channel’s own accounts.

6. Access control and security

API access is restricted to the channel’s single Facebook Page and single Instagram account through a dedicated Meta System User with the minimum permissions required. The channel owner can revoke the App’s access at any time in Meta Business Settings, which immediately ends all API access.

7. Your rights and data deletion

Because the App holds no personal data about followers or viewers, there is normally nothing to delete. If you believe any data relating to you is held, you may request its deletion at any time by following the steps on our Data Deletion Instructions page. Requests are actioned within 30 days and confirmed by email.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: astickintimeofficial@gmail.com.
